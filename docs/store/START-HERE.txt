ADOverride - Chrome Web Store submission kit

1. Open https://chrome.google.com/webstore/devconsole
2. Verify your publisher contact email and complete account requirements.
3. Add a new item. Upload only adoverride-0.11.10-chrome.zip.
4. Follow SUBMISSION.md for listing copy, images, permissions and privacy fields.
5. Review the data-use certifications and arrange a safe ADO reviewer demo.
6. Save the draft. Submit for review only when those items are complete.
